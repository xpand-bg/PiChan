**Status:** FINAL LOCKED

**Production assets:** 2.2

## X / social avatar

Use:

`../vector/PiChan_Circular_Badge.svg`

Render/export from the vector at **1024 × 1024** for master delivery and **400 × 400** for X profile upload.

The badge must contain:

- Deep Navy `#041F5F` circular field
- the canonical full-color PiChan bird from `PiChan_Icon_Master.svg`
- the intentional white circular badge ring
- no white spikes/slivers at the beak
- no decorative white halo around the bird

Do not screenshot the logo, regenerate it with an image model, trace it from a PNG, or use legacy avatar files.

## Production QA sizes

The production 2.2 avatar was visually checked at:

- 400 × 400
- 128 × 128
- 96 × 96
- 64 × 64

At small size, prioritize clean silhouette and recognizable bird features rather than adding detail.
